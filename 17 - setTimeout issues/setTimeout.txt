2. setTimeout drawbacks -
    a. Consider setTimeout.js and it is executed
    b. line-1 executes in GEC @ CALLSTACK, setTimeout's cb() is registered
    c. while loop is started and the main execution thread is withheld
    d. line-12 is executed after while loop is done and then we have the cb() called
    e. This is problematic as the cb() might be important and the CB-Q and event loop waits for the GEC to be popped from the CALLSTACK. CB-Q startvation is happening
    f. Thus setTimeout() has this drawback of relaibility
