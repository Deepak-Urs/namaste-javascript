Functions are the heart of Javascript

Consider the functions.js program --
1. First GEC is created and MEP is done
2. In CEP, x will be assigned 1
3. a() is called and anothe EC is created and pushed to the call stack above the GEC
    a. we print x = 10
    b. a() is popped out of the Call stack
4. b() is called and anothe EC is created and pushed to the call stack above the GEC
    a. we print x = 100
    b. b() is popped out of the Call stack
5. The value of x=1 at the global level is printed as per line-4
6. GEC is popped out of the call stack. End of the program
