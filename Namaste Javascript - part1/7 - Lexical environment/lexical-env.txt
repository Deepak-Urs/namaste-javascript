1. Lexical environment is defined as local memory along with the Lexical environment of its parent/s
2. Scope is the data/functions confined to a block 
3. Scope chain is the link of various Lexical environments
4. In Lexical-env.js -
    a. for c(), Lexical environments are a() and GEC
    b. for a(), Lexical environments are GEC
    c. for main program, we've no Lexical environments and it points to NULL
5. The working of the above code is as follows -
    a. @line-5, b searches for value of b in c() Lexical env., but it won't find it and then searches its parents lex-env
    b. in a() lex-env, we find b and c() accesses it and then prints it out
    c. This chain of search is known as scope chain
